Little-Known Government Program Pays Americans Up to $6,400/Month — Most People Have No Idea It Exists

A federal relief initiative, buried in a 2025 omnibus bill, is sending checks to qualifying Americans — but enrollment closes in 14 days.
By Michael Chen, Fox Business · —
🏛️
The program was quietly included in the American Economic Relief Act of 2025. (AP Photo)

WASHINGTON — While most Americans were focused on election coverage and inflation reports, Congress quietly passed a provision that could put up to $6,400 per month into the bank accounts of millions of qualifying citizens.

The program, officially called the "American Opportunity Direct Payment Initiative" (AODPI), was buried on page 1,847 of the 2025 Consolidated Appropriations Act. It received virtually zero media coverage — until now.

"This is the largest direct-to-citizen payment program since the COVID stimulus checks — but it's ongoing, not a one-time payment. And almost nobody knows about it."
— Rep. James Holloway (R-TX), Ways and Means Committee
