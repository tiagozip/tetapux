Frequently asked

Are free offers real?
Yes — some promotions can make the first year free or nearly free. These are usually limited-time offers or tied to specific TLDs and often come with normal renewal fees starting the following year.
Will I be charged later?
Most free-first-year offers require a paid renewal. Check the domain's renewal rate and any registration restrictions before you accept an offer.
Can I transfer a free domain later?
Yes — domains can generally be transferred, but policies and waiting periods may apply. Transfers often require the domain to be older than 60 days.
Quick tips
  • Search multiple variations — some TLDs are discounted only for select names or categories.
  • Follow Namecheap’s blog, newsletters, or social channels for time-limited coupons.
  • Use the Namecheap coupon field at checkout — community sites sometimes post valid codes.
  • Check TLD rules: country-code TLDs like .co.uk or .ai may have extra eligibility or fees.
