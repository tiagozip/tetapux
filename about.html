What is Tetapux?

Tetapux is collection of fake phishing, scam, adware, fake giveaways and CAPTCHAs, and more typical malvertising pages. It's designed to help users learn to identify phishing pages, and to help protect against phishing attacks, inspired by Hetapus and Hetapugs.

It's quite useful for phishing simulations, and also for learning about phishing and malware.
